# learn.mehmetcinar.com

Mehmet Cinar's public tracker of what he learns from external resources.

## Language

**Learning Path**:
An ordered plan to learn one subject from external resources, such as electricity or Arabic.
_Avoid_: Course (that is one resource), curriculum, track, LMS

**Stage**:
One step of a Learning Path. It groups Courses and ends with a Checkpoint.
_Avoid_: Module, chapter, level

**Course**:
One external resource followed in a Stage: a playlist, a video, a PDF, an online course, a
page. The app links to it and never hosts it.
_Avoid_: Resource, material, lesson, link

**Part**:
One unit of a Course that is finished in a sitting: a video, a chapter, a section. A Course
with no Parts counts as one Part. A Part can be optional; optional Parts never count towards
completion.
_Avoid_: Lesson, item, step

**Checkpoint**:
The statement that proves a Stage is learned, ticked by hand when it holds.
_Avoid_: Quiz, test, milestone

**Progress**:
Which Parts and Checkpoints of a Learning Path are done.
_Avoid_: Status, completion
